Academic Profile

Yifei Zhu

Ph.D. Student

Tohoku University

Yifei Zhu is a Ph.D. student in the Graduate School of Information Sciences at Tohoku University, advised by Professor Yoshifumi Kitamura. He also collaborates with Professor Taku Komura at the University of Hong Kong.

His research lies at the intersection of computer graphics, computer vision, and generative modeling, with a focus on human motion and expressive digital characters.

He develops methods that connect language and speech with coordinated body, hand, and facial motion, aiming to make virtual characters move naturally and communicate expressively.

His work explores the representations and learning dynamics behind motion generation. He is particularly interested in how latent geometry, diffusion, and flow matching shape the quality and controllability of generated motion.

His current research interests include real-time embodied conversation: enabling virtual characters to speak, listen, and move continuously during full-duplex interaction.
